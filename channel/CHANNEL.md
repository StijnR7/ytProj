## Meet Zib
Zib is a cream-coloured probe about the size of a grapefruit, with a navy screen for a face, glowing cyan eyes,
an orange equator band and an antenna with a little beacon. Zib was built to map one asteroid, got curious,
and never went back. Now Zib pokes into every rabbit hole it can find and reports back to you.

Personality: curious, warm, a bit cheeky, easily amazed ("wait... WHAT?"), never condescending.
Zib narrates every video and pops up on screen to react, ask the question the viewer is thinking, and crack the odd joke.

Expressions available to the storyboard: `happy`, `surprised`, `thinking`, `excited`, `worried`, `pointing`
(see `zib_expressions.png`).

## Visual identity
- Palette: navy `#0F1B3D`, orange `#FF7A30`, teal `#2EC4B6`, cyan `#5CF2E6`, yellow `#FFD23F`, cream `#FFF4E0`
- Fonts (bundled, open licence): Luckiest Guy (thumbnails/titles), Fredoka (headings), Nunito (body)
- Thumbnails: 2-4 huge words, one highlighted in yellow, Zib reacting on one side, high-contrast background.
- Each pillar has its own backdrop: starfield (space), parchment (history), jungle (animals), neon grid (hypotheticals).

## Channel "About" text (paste into YouTube)
> Hi, I'm Zib, a tiny probe with a big curiosity problem. Every week I dive into one rabbit hole —
> lost empires, alien-looking animals, the scale of the universe, and wild "what if" scenarios — and come back
> with the full story, explained clearly and backed by sources. Grab a snack. This is going to get weird.

Channel keywords: `documentary, history, space, animals, what if, science explained, education, animated documentary, curiosity`

## Growth notes
- First 30 seconds decide everything: the scripts open with the strongest fact/question and promise a payoff.
- Use **Test & compare** with the 3 generated thumbnails on every upload.
- Cut 2-3 Shorts per video from the strongest moments (ideas are generated in the upload checklist) and link them to the long video.
- Group videos into playlists by pillar so binge-watching kicks in.
